### 7. Public Commissions Established by the 1979 and Subsequent Constitutions

#### Subtopics
- **Civil Service Commission**: Objectives (merit-based recruitment); functions (appointments, promotions, discipline); problems (political interference).
- **Public Complaints Commission**: Objectives (redress maladministration); functions (investigate complaints, recommend remedies); problems (lack of enforcement).
- **Electoral Commissions**: Objectives (free/fair elections); functions (voter registration, conduct polls); problems (bias allegations, logistics).
- **National Boundary Commission**: Objectives (resolve disputes); functions (demarcate boundaries, mediate); problems (slow resolution).
- **Others (e.g., Federal Character Commission)**: Objectives (equitable representation); functions (monitor appointments); problems (mediocrity over merit).

#### Learning Objectives
Candidates should follow this orderly sequence:
1. Identify the public commissions and their establishment under 1979/1999 Constitutions.
2. Evaluate their operations (objectives and functions in governance).
3. Assess problems and constraints (e.g., independence issues for INEC, delays in boundary resolutions).
4. Analyze impact (e.g., how these commissions support democratic principles like accountability and federalism).
