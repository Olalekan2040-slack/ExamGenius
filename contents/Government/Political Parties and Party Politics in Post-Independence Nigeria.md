### 8. Political Parties and Party Politics in Post-Independence Nigeria

#### Subtopics
- **First Republic (1960–1966)**: Evolution (regional/ethnic bases); membership spread (NPC North, NCNC East, AG West); structure (hierarchical, elite-led); composition (ethnic/regional elites).  
- **Second Republic (1979–1983)**: Evolution (national spread attempts post-civil war); membership spread (NPN broad, UPN Yoruba); structure (more national, but money-influenced); composition (elite-dominated).  
- **Third Republic (Aborted 1993)**: Evolution (imposed two-party system by Babangida); membership spread (forced national); structure (government-controlled); composition (screened candidates).  
- **Fourth Republic (1999–present)**: Evolution (multi-party explosion, PDP dominance 1999–2015, APC rise 2015+); membership spread (national but ethnic influences); structure (NEC, NWC, state/ward levels); composition (personality/ethnic-driven).

#### Learning Objectives
Candidates should follow this orderly sequence:
1. Contrast political processes in the republics (e.g., regional rivalry in First vs. national coalitions in Second).
2. Evaluate the ideologies (often weak/personality-based), structure (hierarchical/national), and composition (elite/ethnic) of political parties.
3. Assess impact on democracy (e.g., multi-party in Fourth promotes representation but causes instability).
